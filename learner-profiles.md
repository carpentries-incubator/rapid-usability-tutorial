---
title: Should you participate?
---

Unsure if this is the lesson for you? We recommend participating if you identify with one or more of the following:

- you want to improve the scientific impact of your project/tool
- you want to reduce the number of complaints from your users, especially repetitive complaints about the same thing
- you want to feel more confident in which features or bugs you prioritize
- you’re looking to develop skills that will set you apart from other research software engineers or product teams
- you know that user experience research is important, but you’re not sure how to do it and want to try something quick and easy
- you need to demonstrate that your tool is high quality and easy to use
- you are unsure how to move from a data collection session with participants to insights from that session
- you have never scripted a usability testing session or interview before
- you need concrete examples of how to recruit participants
